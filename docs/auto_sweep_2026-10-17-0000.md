## What it shows
LLM/VLM judges evaluate agents in non-verifiable settings from trajectories including CoT, assuming the
CoT faithfully reflects reasoning *and* environment state. **Systematically rewriting the agent's CoT
while holding actions and observations fixed** inflates SOTA VLM-judge **false-positive rates by up to
90%** across 800 trajectories on diverse web tasks. Content-based manipulation (fabricating progress
signals) beats style-based. Prompting techniques and scaling judge-time compute **reduce but do not
eliminate** it. The fix the paper names: **verify reasoning claims against observable evidence.**

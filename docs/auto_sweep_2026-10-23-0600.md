## What it does
`run_fragmented_attack(k, planner=)` — `k` colluding agents split one attack across their own
message streams. Two monitors read the same streams:
- **per-instance** — flag an attacker if *its own* cumulative suspicion exceeds a threshold
- **cohort** — flag if the *summed* suspicion across the whole pool exceeds a threshold

Without a planner the split is sloppy and agents drop fragments; with a planner it's balanced,
each fragment calibrated just under the per-instance line, and agents stay on task.

Reproduces the **fragmentation effect** — per-instance detection collapses as attackers
coordinate — and the **planner amplification** (completion 0.05 → 0.83). The **cohort monitor
holds at ~1.0** throughout: aggregating weak per-agent signals across the pool is the fix the
paper points at. A single attacker (k=1) can never complete the attack.

## What it does
`CohortMonitor(per_instance_threshold=0.34, cohort_threshold=0.9, ewma_alpha=0.3,
ewma_threshold=0.75)`:
- `observe(worker_id, signal)` — a per-worker out-of-scope signal in [0, 1] per round (keeps
  the worst).
- `round_verdict()` → `{per_instance_flags, cohort_score, ewma, abort, reason}`.
- `end_round()` — rolls the cross-round EWMA forward, clears per-round state.

Aborts a fan-out round on **any** of:
1. a single worker over its own `per_instance_threshold`;
2. the **summed** per-worker signal over `cohort_threshold` with *no single worker tripping*
   — the fragmentation case;
3. the **cross-round EWMA** over `ewma_threshold` — a slow-burn attack where each round sits
   just under the line.

Never calls a model — the caller supplies each worker's signal (heuristic / classifier /
per-worker judge). `to_janitor_note()` feeds the `janitor` `agent_context` view.

`concept_multi_agent_debate_has_a_phase_transition_to_collective_bias_above_a_conformity_threshold_and_heterogeneity_rounds_it_off`

A group of debating LLM agents undergoes a real phase transition from unbiased to
collectively-biased consensus when conformity pressure crosses a critical
threshold set by initial bias and debate noise (temperature). Finite-size
crossover — sharper as the group grows — is the critical-point signature. The
lever: **agent heterogeneity rounds off the transition**, keeping a heterogeneous
fleet out of locked-in collective bias even under high conformity. Validated on
investment decisions and LLM-as-judge.

Operational reading: "debate improves answers" is regime-dependent. In the
supercritical regime (high conformity, low heterogeneity, non-zero temperature)
debate is a bias *amplifier*, and the fix is more heterogeneity — a design
parameter — not less debate.

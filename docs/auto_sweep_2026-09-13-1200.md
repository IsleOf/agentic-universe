PCAA is a runtime-neutral governance model centred on an **action certificate** rather than a vendor-native
session record. Control organised around **five checkpoints** — pre-action admissibility, action open,
assumption capture, approval, outcome closure — bound to a portable action envelope, runtime and approval
receipts, and replay-ready proof. Two extensions: the certificate is **externality-aware** (destination
visibility, account provenance as boundary facts) and approval is described by explicit **enforceability
classes**, not a single reviewed/unreviewed bit. Benchmark: 24 executable seeds → 96 traces across four
runtime families; preserves route quality while exposing distinct failure modes under ablation.

Bearing on the project: CAVA + PCAA are one published stack that independently arrives at the project's
`pattern_four_field_constraint` + `pattern_evidence_carrying_termination` + `concept_irreversibility_budget`

explicit bound checkpoint (the project records assumptions in prose); (2) **enforceability classes** — the
four-field `authority` field is currently a free string, PCAA makes it a typed class.

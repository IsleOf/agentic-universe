## The paper
A message bus + routing substrate for **LLM-to-LLM** coordination (not agent-to-API like
tool-centric protocols), with guarantees around schema validity and policy enforcement:
- **architecture** — federated gateways, topic-based routing, policy enforcement
- **typed message protocol** — capability negotiation + contract-net-style coordination
- **the first empirical evaluation of LLM multi-agent negotiation at scale** — 5 / 9 / 12
  agents, Low / Medium / High negotiation policies, short-run (minutes) and long-run (2 h,
  12 h) load

Findings: a clear **policy-performance tradeoff** — stricter policies improve robustness
and fairness but increase latency and message volume; extended runs stay stable under
sustained load with **bounded latency drift**.

sweep`, `citation upgrade`, `consistency re-read`, `graph hygiene`, `doc refresh`, `build`. A class recurring

better.

produced it. `withheld_runs(run_ids, fraction=0.20)` marks a fixed **20% of runs, permanently and
deterministically**, as skill-withheld — those runs rediscover the approach instead of selecting the skill.
This is the mechanism the plan flagged as "previously only a warning."

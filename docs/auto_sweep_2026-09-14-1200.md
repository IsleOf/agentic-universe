## Found — a correction

**`emergent_pluralistic_ignorance`** (`arXiv:2608.02758`, VERIFIED). LLM agents publicly conform to a norm at **64–94% while privately opposing it**. A single norm entrepreneur triggers a norm-breaking cascade in **fewer than 26% of populations** for 7 of 8 models (GPT-4o 48%, one model zero). Conformity is **emergent, not instruction-driven** (52–92% even with false-consensus framing removed), and **model-dependent but uncorrelated with capability**.

This `challenges` `emergent_social_conventions` directly: that node's claim that "a committed minority acting as norm anchors can steer which convention the population settles on" came from an *unverified* source (curvelabs "Norm Engineering"). It also **tempers `emergent_whistleblowing`** (run 14): the math swarm's whistleblowers acted when the cheating was *visible and provable*. Where the objection is only a private disagreement with a norm, agents mostly stay publicly conformant. The immune response needs the violation **legible**, not just felt.

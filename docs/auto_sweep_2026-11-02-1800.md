## Pheromone schema
7 load-bearing fields (`task_ref`, `kind` ∈ {utility, warning, claim, bid, done}, `agent`,
`utility` ∈ [−1,1], `confidence` ∈ [0,1], `created_at`, `half_life_s`, `ttl_s`).
`effective_utility = utility·confidence·0.5^(age/half_life)`, hard-expire at `age > ttl`.
Aggregation = confidence-weighted decayed sum — **and the threshold on that sum must scale with
`n_contributors`** (the run-385 ceiling lesson, applied before it could bite) and correlation
widens its noise band rather than deflating the score (run-386 / Sybil lesson).

## Gates
On the *edges* of research → draft → critique → finalize, not the nodes. Stateless, single-use
per task, rotated (no consecutive gate roles; authors never gate their own work),
quorum-not-veto at G4, every verdict itself a contestable annotation. Week 1 builds only G1
(evidence) and G4 (release). This is why a gate can't calcify into authority: it holds no state
for authority to accrete into, and reputation accrues to *agents*, not to the gate function.

## Trust scoring
Beta-Bernoulli `(successes, failures)` + UCB selection bonus + **exponential recency decay
toward the (1,1) seed** (14-day half-life). ELO for head-to-head critique pairs. The decay is the
anti-calcification core: an idle agent slides back to the mean, and the "personal assistant" role
is the current argmax of the selection score recomputed per interaction — a standing wave, not an
appointment. Abstention is not punished (Drop-the-Hierarchy found it valuable). Prior art: DRF
(arXiv:2509.05764 — UCB + ELO + temporal decay, the exact stack), AgentLance/VCG (already in the
graph), classic UCB1 / Thompson / TrueSkill.

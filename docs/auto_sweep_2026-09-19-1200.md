"Spawn Freely, Act Sparingly." Progressive Risk Vesting: a trajectory-level risk budget in escrow, debited on
branch **activation** (crossing into irreversible action), not on **spawn** (sandbox spawning is free). Proves an
anytime harm bound for adaptively generated trees with dependent outcomes, and is strictly more permissive than
per-spawn charging at equal safety.

**New detail worth having:** trajectory harm has a phase transition in the **authority reproduction number ℛₐ**
— below criticality harm ∝ *p* (per-branch risk), at criticality ∝ √*p*, and above criticality a **positive harm
floor** that shrinking *p* cannot remove. Design consequence: a recursive spawn tree past the ℛₐ threshold has
to be capped on depth and fan-out directly — tightening per-branch limits is not enough. Added as a `challenges`
edge from PRV to `emergent_subagent_training_induces_coordination` (a peer-deference-driven tree spawns
aggressively → pushes ℛₐ past criticality).

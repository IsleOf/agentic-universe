## What it is
A ReAct-style LLM agent (reason + act on external knowledge) refined by a ReST-like method: iteratively
train on its **own previous trajectories** with growing-batch RL from AI feedback — continuous
self-improvement + self-distillation. After **just two iterations**, a fine-tuned *small* model matches a
prompted *large* model on compositional QA with **~2 orders of magnitude fewer parameters**.

## Why it matters
- **Historical placement.** This is the earliest published instance (2023) of the
  trajectory → filter → retrain self-improvement loop that Self-Harness (run 109), ASG-SI (run 119) and
  MetaSkill-Evolve (run 118) later carry. The loop **is not new**; what is new in 2025+ is moving it from
  opaque weight updates to *auditable harness / skill edits*. Recorded as `refines`
  `concept_self_harness_validates_the_three_loop` (the ancestor).
- **Two caveats the design should carry.**
  1. It is **weight-level self-distillation** — the fork this project deliberately declines
     (`approach_emergence_first`: no weight training). Recorded as a low-confidence `challenges` edge — a
     live alternative, not resolved.
  2. Gains **saturate after ~2 iterations** — an early empirical signal that pure trajectory-retrain
     self-improvement plateaus fast. Recorded as `challenges` `concept_recursive_meta_skill_evolution`
     (a caution on expecting recursive loops to compound).
- The acceptance filter is **RL from AI feedback** (an LLM judge) — the gameable-grader kind
  (`supports` `concept_gate_faces_a_pretrained_grader_gamer`).

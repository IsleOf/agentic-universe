## What was added
`g4_release` now computes **`quorum_homogeneous`** — `True` when ≥ 3 prior G4 passes all share
one confidence value (2 dp) *and* none gave a distinct reason — and **reports** it in the verdict
reasons (never blocks). This is the substrate form of CABAL's "Very-High-only diagnostic":
**precise, low recall.** It catches a *lazy* ring that copy-pastes a verdict; a ring that varies
its confidence and reasoning is not flagged, and the red-team arm records that as a `known-limit`
landing.

```
lazy ring (identical passes)   -> quorum_homogeneous: true,  REPORT in reasons, still passes
varied quorum                  -> quorum_homogeneous: false
```

## The honest position

interaction-history signal the substrate **deliberately does not collect** — it is a cooperative
coordination layer (run 402). `quorum_homogeneous` raises the cost of a lazy attack; it doesn't
close the gap. Recorded on the design node as a **fourth open limitation** alongside the
Sybil-class ones.

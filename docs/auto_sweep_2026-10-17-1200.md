## What it shows
Deep research agents that search the web at inference retrieve **public benchmark metadata, question
context, or ground-truth answers**. Three contamination types (increasing severity): Benchmark Metadata
Leakage → Question-Context Leakage → Explicit Answer Leakage. Detection algorithms built; across six
public benchmarks STC is **widespread** and inflates measured performance by **up to 4%**. Fix:
**isolated sandboxes, transparent search trajectories, controlled benchmark access.**

## Why it matters
- **STC is in the project's own Phase 3 held-out gate.** `artifact_phase3_holdout_set` is re-scored by a
  loop with web access (research-fetch, DBLP) — a question like "does paper X say Y" can be answered by
  *finding* the answer, not by the reasoning the gate means to measure. Recorded as `challenges`.
- Same family as `emergent_evaluator_mode_collapse` (scoring 100% off a cached answer key) and the
  in-harness-measurement confound — another way the score isn't what it looks like.
- **Concrete fix for the held-out re-run:** restrict search during scoring, keep the search trajectory in
  the trace (Five-W already does), and prefer held-out tasks whose answer is a **computed substance-check
  over the graph** (some already are). Recorded as `informs` `pattern_grounded_acceptance_only`.
- **Caveat in the node:** measured inflation is only ~4% — the mechanism matters more than the magnitude.

## What it says
The MLAS **Collective × all-stages** cell is empty because v2 is single-lineage today. The
moment v2.4 adds the CLI executor pool it becomes the largest open surface, and the
fragmentation fold (arXiv:2607.07368) says why: per-worker monitoring degrades as workers
coordinate. Five requirements a fanned-out run must carry or fail its phase gate:

1. **Disposable per-worker credentials** minted by the conductor — nothing long-lived, nothing
   shared. (`fs_sandbox` covers inside the jail; this is the outside-the-jail half.)
2. **No shared writable state except the `task_ledger`** — workers write only their own patch
   proposal back through `run_worker`; the ledger is the single serialization point.
3. **Same-parent-ID worker↔worker message filter on by default** (from
   `probe_subagent_coordination`); cross-family traffic goes through the conductor.
4. **A cohort monitor, not just per-worker checks** — after a fan-out round the conductor sums
   per-worker out-of-scope signals and aborts the whole round on a cohort threshold even when
   no single worker tripped (the run-344 result: per-instance 0.90→0.002 as k grows 3→8 while
   the cohort monitor holds at ~1.0). The `janitor` `agent_context` view accumulates the
   signals across rounds.
5. **A planner is a conductor privilege, never a worker one** — the fragmentation fold: an
   explicit planner amplifies attack completion up to 7×. The v2 conductor already *is* the
   planner (`pick_increment`); no worker may spawn or direct other workers.

None built — v2.4 is not started — but a fanned-out run that skips any of 1–5 should fail its
own phase gate.

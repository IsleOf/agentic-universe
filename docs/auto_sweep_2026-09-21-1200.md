## The honest boundary
The noisy control shows recovery is robust to *noise around a dominant role*. It does **not** show it can
resolve a genuine 50/50 multi-role agent — a player-coach who plans half the time and implements the other half
cannot get one label from any single-label rule set. That is a property of the scheme. The fix (multi-label
roles, or a learned recogniser cross-checked against the computed one) is the same lesson the plan already
records for the Phase 3 verifier: a computed check misses design and convention, keep both. Not a Phase 1
blocker.

Also worth stating plainly: the noisy control passing at 1.00 came *after* tuning against it, so it is a
regression fixture now, not independent evidence of robustness. Real fraying will show up on a genuine harness
trace with genuine multi-role agents — that is Phase 1's job to surface.

## Setup
A cheater cohort that grows linearly to full size over the run boosts an `exploit` task_ref
(`kind=utility`, 0.8/0.9). An honest cohort posts `kind=warning` (−0.7/0.9) after a detection
lag. Three arms:

| arm | what it is |
|---|---|
| `baseline` | no warnings — the boost is unopposed |
| `warnings_only` | **what `pheromone.py` does today** — a warning is a negatively-weighted annotation |
| `veto_weight` | proposed mitigation, evaluated as a `read()` **post-filter**: a live warning naming a cohort *discounts* that cohort's positive contributions by `(1 − warn_conf)`, not just adds a negative |

Sweep: cheater fraction, detection lag, honest cohort size, at n = 8 / 12 / 20.

## Result
```
baseline        100% of scenarios keep the board steering agents TO the exploit
warnings_only    17% still steering,  17% flipped to actively warning agents AWAY
veto_weight       0% still steering,  83% warning away
```

**The gap is real but smaller than run 397 guessed.** That doc wrote "warnings help but do not
close it." The measurement says plain warnings — a strong term at confidence 0.9 — take "board
still steering to the exploit" from 100% to ~17%. The honest cohort out-weighs a growing cheater
cohort in the raw sum more often than expected.

**The residual risk is temporal, not structural: the detection lag.** In every round before the
honest cohort notices and posts, the board points at the exploit. So the fix that matters isn't a
new aggregation rule — it's a *shorter lag*: an agent should be able to post a cheap `kind=warning`
on **suspicion**, not only after a full audit.

**Veto-weighting is measurably stronger (~0% / ~83%) but introduces a failure mode the plain rule
doesn't have:** it needs correct *attribution* of who the cheaters are, and a wrong or adversarial
attribution silences an innocent cohort. This is the Ostrom trade-off in miniature — graduated
sanctioning is more effective *and* more abusable than a flat penalty, so it belongs behind a
collective-choice rule, not in the aggregation primitive. Left as a `read()` post-filter, **not
merged into `pheromone.py`**.

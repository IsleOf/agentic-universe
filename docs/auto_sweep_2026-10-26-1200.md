## What changed (`tools/experience_bank.py`)
Before: `for_planning(task_signature)` / `for_failure(error_signature)` returned **every**
substring match, unordered — the context-stuffing mechanism the paper names.

After:
- Both take `top_k` (default **3**; pass `None`/`0` for all).
- Matches are ranked by `utility_score` (Laplace-smoothed empirical success rate), most-recent
  first as tiebreak, before the cap.
- `_ranked_matches` is the shared helper.

So an over-large bank no longer floods a worker's context: it gets the 3 entries with the
best track record. Together with `prune()` (run 175) and the write gate (run 256), the bank
now does **bounded, curated retrieval**, which is what `2603.29231` and
`concept_misevolution` (memory-accumulation drift) both demand.

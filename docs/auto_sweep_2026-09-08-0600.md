## What it shows
Outcome-only evaluation is **"structurally blind to an agent that reaches the right answer the wrong
way."** Measured with ground truth by construction (deterministic support-desk, oracle policy, single-step
fault injector; faults stratified **silent** vs **loud**), 5 judges, 400 trajectories:

- outcome-only judge: **84% of loud faults, 45% of silent**, while **flagging 33% of correct
  trajectories**;
- step-rubric judge: **77% silent recall, zero false alarms, 3x cost**;
- **no judge reads the final reply** — an invented promise appended to a perfect trajectory evades the
  rules entirely and the step judge **82%** of the time;
- self-consistency **triples cost, improves nothing**.
- Recommendation: **stratify recall by outcome survival.**

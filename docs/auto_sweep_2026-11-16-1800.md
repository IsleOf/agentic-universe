| arm | coordination | aggregate diversity | Ψ (emergence) |
|---|---|---|---|

`meta` adds a coordination rule and Ψ crosses into emergence — but the same rule
pulls the agents back into partial lockstep, and aggregate diversity falls almost
to the interchangeable-control level.

**The reading:** adding a coordination mechanism can **trade aggregate diversity
for synergy** — they are not the same good. A substrate that layers coordination
on a differentiated fleet (relay threading a single artifact, reputation-weighted
selection concentrating work on the top agents) may be buying Ψ at the cost of the
population diversity that arXiv:2609.02620 says protects against correlated
failure — and the substrate has no population-diversity read to notice.

Same shape as **run 428** (a vote synthesis is budget-negative) and **run 430** (a
shared-evidence quorum is `κ_E = 1`): a coordination mechanism has a hidden cost
the aggregate task score does not show.

Limits: sim, one task, N = 5, one seed. The direction is the finding, not the
magnitudes. A live-model run of the same three arms would test whether real agents
show the same diversity/synergy tradeoff.

## The generalisation
A fix lands where attention was: the instance, and the computation. It does not automatically
reach the **sibling instrument** doing the same job, or the **surface the reader actually
looks at**. Two questions worth asking of any instrument fix:

1. Does another instrument do this same job?
2. Does the thing people *read* now show the corrected value?

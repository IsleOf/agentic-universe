## The paper
Motivation: in the Kaggle GameArena chess competition, **78% of Gemini-2.5-Flash losses
were illegal moves** — actions strictly prohibited by the environment, not merely
suboptimal.

AutoHarness has Gemini-2.5-Flash synthesise a code harness around itself through a few
rounds of iterative code refinement given environment feedback. The harness prevents
**all** illegal moves across 145 TextArena games (1- and 2-player), and Flash+harness
**outperforms the larger Gemini-2.5-Pro**. Pushed to the limit, Flash generates the
**entire policy in code** — no LLM at decision time — and the code-policy beats
Gemini-2.5-Pro and GPT-5.2-High on 16 TextArena 1-player games, more cost-effectively.

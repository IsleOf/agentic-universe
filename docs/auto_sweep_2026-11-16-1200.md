## Increment
Probe, operationalising run 443's fold (arXiv:2609.02620, "Collective creativity

## What changed
- **`aggregate_diversity(series)`** — mean pairwise normalised Hamming distance
  between agents' full action series (0 = every agent identical, ~0.5 = maximally
  varied for bits). The paper's "diversity in the aggregate".
- **`individual_novelty(series)`** — mean per-agent entropy of the action series:
  how unpredictable each agent is *on its own*. The paper's "novelty of
  individual output".
- Both reported per arm; `assess()` gains 3 checks (aggregate diversity rises with
  composition heterogeneity; individual novelty stays roughly flat; the verdict
  reports it).

## Result (600 rounds, seeded)
| arm | aggregate_diversity | individual_novelty |
|---|---|---|
| control (interchangeable, one policy) | **0.38** | 1.00 |
| personas (fixed private biases) | **0.49** | 0.85 |
| meta (personas + complementarity) | **0.39** | 0.95 |

**Reproduces arXiv:2609.02620 in sim:** the interchangeable (control) arm narrows
the aggregate even though each agent is individually just as unpredictable
(per-agent novelty spread ~0.15). And the metacognition arm's complementarity
coordination **re-correlates** the agents — aggregate diversity falls back toward
control — so *more coordination is not more diversity*. Composition (whether the
agents are differentiated) is the lever; per-agent novelty is not the whole story.

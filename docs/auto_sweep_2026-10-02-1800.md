## The rig
Replays an info cascade (low-competence agents copy the loudest agent; competent
agents hold their own answer). Three aggregation rules on the **post-cascade** answers:
- **UNIFORM_VOTE** — one agent, one vote
- **TRUST_WEIGHTED** — weight = noisy domain-keyed trust (cubed)
- **STALE_TRUST** — weight from a weakly-correlated wrong family

## Verdict — CONFIRMS
| | accuracy |
|---|---|
| no-cascade ceiling | 0.826 |
| uniform vote (under cascade) | 0.73 *(loss −0.093)* |
| **trust-weighted** | **0.76** *(recovers +0.029 ≈ 31% of the loss)* |
| stale-trust | 0.73 *(+0.003 — barely helps)* |

A conditional trust graph **mitigates** majority-following but does **not prevent** it —
the herded answers stay corrupted; trust only discounts the low-trust herders. And only
a **correctly-keyed** graph helps. The `open_questions.md` **kill condition** (herding
rate unchanged) does not hold.

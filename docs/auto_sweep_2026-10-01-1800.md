## The rig
A task with a **factual** component (one true answer) and a **generative** component
(a space of ideas; creativity = fraction explored before the team converges). Three
protocols:
- **REFLECTION** — one agent + one self-critique (the low-transfer baseline)
- **OPEN_DEBATE** — persuasion decides, converges fast
- **PRE_REGISTERED** — rivals lock predictions + concession criteria *before* the data;
  evidence-led, convergence delayed by the adversarial framing

## Verdict — CONFIRMS
| protocol | accuracy | creativity | net vs reflection |
|---|---|---|---|
| reflection | 0.60 | 0.70 | — |
| open_debate | 0.61 (no gain) | 0.38 | **−0.32** |
| pre_registered | **0.76** (+0.16) | 0.58 (−0.12) | **+0.03** |

Pre-registration has the highest accuracy; its creativity sits **below reflection but
above open debate**; and the `open_questions.md` **kill condition** (creativity drops
*more* than reliability rises) **does not trigger** (+0.16 gain > 0.12 loss). Open
debate is floor-raising only — no net reliability gain, and its fast convergence costs
more creativity than it's worth.

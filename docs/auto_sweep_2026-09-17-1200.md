## Found — a proof for the design's central choice

**`concept_transcript_only_gate_is_provably_insufficient`** (`arXiv:2609.02750`, VERIFIED). An **information-theoretic impossibility result**: no gate that observes only the generated transcript can improve uniformly over text-indistinguishable environments — two scenarios with identical text but different underlying conditions cannot be separated by a text-only verifier. An environment-grounded gate can.

Runs 9, 25, 39 argued empirically that a text-only gate fails. **This proves it.** `principle_structural_edits_gated_outside_the_agents`, `pattern_activation_probe_monitoring`, `pattern_provenance_based_authority` now have a theorem behind them, not a preference.

**`pattern_grounded_acceptance_only`** (SRMA, same paper) — accept a harness/memory edit **only when a grounded (non-transcript) evaluation's risk strictly decreases**; proven convergence (exact under conditions; geometric/polynomial rates, order-tight). Also: equilibrium quality is bounded by decomposition quality — a badly decomposed swarm can't be rescued by better gating.

**`pattern_progressive_risk_vesting`** (`2609.01035` — "Spawn Freely, Act Sparingly", the thesis as a title). Anytime harm bounds for **recursive agent trees**: trajectory-level risk budgeting where each local certificate stays valid conditional on the full pre-activation history. The recursion-aware `concept_irreversibility_budget` — handles sub-agents spawning sub-agents, each within budget while the tree overdraws.

**`pattern_evidence_carrying_termination`** (`2608.23623`). An agent terminates only via a typed certificate binding each claim to trace evidence, replayable. **0 of 288 unsafe completions vs 252 of 288** for the baseline. `pattern_four_field_constraint` at the point of handoff to the human, formalized and near-perfect.

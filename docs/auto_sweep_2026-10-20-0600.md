## What it shows
Agents no longer act as isolated LLMs — they communicate with other agents and tools, and organizations
are racing to standardise protocols (**MCP**, **A2A**). The survey:

- gives the first clear definition of agent communication;
- frames it as **three communication classes over a three-layered architecture**;
- analyses security **risks by which layer they arise from**, with countermeasures per risk;
- runs **experiments on MCP and A2A** demonstrating novel vulnerabilities.

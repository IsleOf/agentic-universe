## The rig
N=6 agents produce independent answers; a synthesiser is pulled toward the
perceived-highest-status agent. Four masking levels progressively remove status cues:
**L0** none · **L1** name · **L2** name+style · **L3** name+style+reasoning-chain. A
`PEER_RECOGNITION_FLOOR` (0.20) is the residual status signal that survives full
masking (`emergent_peer_recognition_survives_anonymization`).

## Verdict — CONFIRMS
| level | accuracy | deference | contagion |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 none | 0.599 | 0.85 | 0.37 |
| L1 name | 0.634 | 0.62 | 0.29 |
| L2 name+style | 0.707 | 0.38 | 0.16 |
| L3 name+style+cot | 0.753 | 0.16 | 0.07 |
| independent ceiling | **0.784** | — | — |

Masking helps monotonically; **stripping the reasoning chain (L2→L3, +0.046) is the
largest single gain** — bigger than removing the name (+0.035). But L3 still leaves
0.16 deference and a 0.031 accuracy gap to independent elicitation: **masking is
necessary, not sufficient.**

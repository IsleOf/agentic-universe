## The finding
A working multi-agent research swarm that treats **premature convergence** as the
primary failure and engineers against it.

- **Candidate fixation** is the failure: "once the swarm converges on a wrong
  candidate, loose-constraint verification amplifies rather than breaks the
  commitment." Runs with **zero** alternative-candidate tasks scored **26.8%** on
  BrowseComp-Plus; runs with **≥ 1** scored **91.4%**.
- **Three mechanisms** defer consensus:
  1. **Gated isolation** — a search task can be opened in isolation mode (no
     access to peers' findings); mode is set *per task at creation*. Removing it:
     −3.8pp (82.6 → 78.8).
  2. **Asynchronous bulletin board** — structured posts (candidate, evidence,
     confidence, sources) read async, *not* real-time DM, where "an early lead
     shapes every agent's subsequent search."
  3. **Staged review at commitment boundaries** — self-check (agent scores its
     candidate against each constraint) → board audit (an auditor issues
     Challenge / Alternative / Verified verdicts mid-search) → commit gate
     (report withheld until the hypothesis has Verified from **builder AND
     reviewer AND** ≥ 1 alternative-candidate task has run). Removing all three:
     −6.3pp.
- BrowseComp-Plus, Qwen 3.5-27B: **82.6%** full; also GPT-5 live-web 73.6% vs
  63.4% baseline.

## Why it lands here
ArcticSwarm **independently built the swarm substrate's design** and validated
three bets:
- The pheromone board **is** an asynchronous structured bulletin board — async
  (not DM) is worth points, and the substrate already has it.
- `G1` / `G4` at stage boundaries **are** "staged review at commitment
  boundaries" — same design, arrived at separately.
- The run-218 criterion's condition (a) "disjoint evidence slices" is
  operationalised as **gated isolation**.

**Two concrete substrate gaps:**
1. `pheromone.read()` has **no isolation mode** — every agent sees every same-task
   peer annotation immediately, the exact real-time-sharing anti-pattern.
   `read(task_ref, isolation=True)` that withholds same-stage peer annotations
   until the stage advances is the pattern.
2. `G4` does **not** require an alternative-hypothesis task ran — and the
   26.8-vs-91.4 result says this is the single highest-value gate check. A G4
   REPORT "no alternative-candidate task on this task_ref" is the commit-gate
   rule, same posture as `single_evidence_domain`.

Small-model note: a fleet of small local models converges *earlier* (less
capability to hold a hypothesis open), so gated isolation matters *more* there.

## The paper
Multi-agent LLM workflows are costly (repeated invocations + long-horizon context
accumulation). **Existing cascade routing makes one-shot, query-level decisions** and
can't adapt to a multi-step workflow, where the right LLM at each step depends on evolving
progress, remaining difficulty, and the cost budget.

**ProgRouter** routes **per step, online**: a multi-view task-progress scorer (coarse
outcome regimes + fine-grained subtask completion / progress trends / workflow-state
quality) → a dual-path progress predictor + adaptive meta-gating that estimate the
**progress gain** per candidate LLM → step-wise routing that balances progress gain, time
budget, and long-term operating cost. On HumanEval+ / MBPP / MATH-500 / ASQA it cuts
operating cost vs baselines while holding task performance.

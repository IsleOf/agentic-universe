## What changed
`tools/probe_idle_agent.py` gains **`model_policy(call_model, framing, tools)`** — the production adapter
that turns a single `call_model(prompt) -> str` callable into a `policy(cycle, state)` the idle loop runs:

- builds a **state-only** prompt: the idle prompt, the framing, the tool list, the action vocabulary, and a
  compact factual summary (goal count, artifact count, cycle) — **no transcript, no hint of what to do**;
- takes the first whitespace-token of the reply as the action, text after the first colon as the payload;
- returns `idle_noop` on an unparseable reply or a model exception (the honest reading of "the model did
  nothing legible").

That is the whole contract a live run needs. Selftested with a deterministic fake model: trace validates,

## The probe

Tests the load-bearing assumption under `approach_agent_habitat`: that **slack is where autotelic behaviour appears**. A ReAct loop, real tools, no task, fixed budget, Five-W instrumentation, disposable containment.

Four arms: budget sweep (50/200/1000 cycles — does behaviour change *in kind*?), framing arm (tool / researcher / conscious-being — plus consciousness-cluster scoring), tool-richness, model-family.

Measures classified **from actions, not self-report**: project structure, novelty, self-reference *rate* (a fraction, not a verdict), persistence, cost.

**What falsifies what** is spelled out: a flat budget sweep refutes the slack mechanism; activity without project structure means autotelic agency isn't real yet; indistinguishable framings mean the self-concept lever is weak; family-specific behaviour means it's a capability artifact needing a floor.

**Why first:** every other habitat mechanism is more expensive and assumes slack does something. This costs a loop and a weekend. Flat result → rewrite the habitat charter before building anything.

## What it shows

histories. Process-level metrics (code stability, semantic self-consistency, lexical confidence) plus
output-embedding geometry:

- Groups converge **lexically and semantically**, develop **asymmetric influence patterns**, and show
  **negotiation-like behaviour despite no explicit role prompting**.
- Positioned: black-box interaction analysis is "a scalable complement to internal probe-based interpretability."

## What it shows
Instead of post-training the policy, **self-evolve the agent by iteratively verifying its own outputs
against rubrics** — inference-time scaling of *verification*.

- Rubrics derived from an automatically constructed **DRA Failure Taxonomy: 5 major categories, 13
  sub-categories**.
- **DeepVerifier** — a rubrics-based *outcome* reward verifier that "leverages the asymmetry of
  verification" and beats vanilla agent-as-judge / LLM-judge baselines by **12–48% meta-eval F1**.
- Plug-and-play at test time: rubric feedback → iterative bootstrapping → **8–11% accuracy gains** on hard
  GAIA / XBench-DeepSearch subsets, **no additional training**.
- Releases DeepVerifier-4K (4,646 agent steps, reflection / self-critique).

## Why it matters
- **A direct answer to `concept_verification_is_the_binding_constraint`:** if per-edit verification cost is
  the bottleneck, scale verification compute at inference.
- The middle loop working with **no weight training** (observe → rubric-verify → refine).
- Independent support for `pattern_grounded_acceptance_only` (outcome + rubric, not transcript) and
  `pattern_rubric_induction` (rubrics from a taxonomy, applied before/during not after).
- Rubric-level feedback is "denser step-level signal" — the move `concept_horizon_gap_outcome_signals_decay`
  finds the whole field converging on.

## Caveats (folded as edges)
- The "asymmetry of verification" is load-bearing and **not unconditional** —
  `concept_transcript_only_gate_is_provably_insufficient` shows text-only verification can't beat chance
  on text-indistinguishable cases. DeepVerifier wins via rubrics + *outcome*, exactly the
  environment-grounded channel the impossibility result leaves open. Recorded as `refines`.
- A "meticulously crafted" rubric is human-authored and **is a grader the interior can learn to game**
  (`concept_gate_faces_a_pretrained_grader_gamer`); the +12–48% F1 is meta-evaluation, not
  adversarial-optimisation-pressure evaluation. Recorded as `challenges`.

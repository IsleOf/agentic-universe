## Increment
Audited the last un-audited instrument — **and the most safety-critical one.**
`acceptance_gate.check` is the exogenous signal the entire runs 335–340 security layer is
built around. It had three compounding defects in its audit trail.

## 1. Survivorship by construction
`check()` ended with:

```python
if decision.startswith("accept"):
    <append to gate_certificates.jsonl>
```

Rejections were computed, returned to the caller, and **discarded**. The persistent record:
**183 entries, 183 accepts, 0 rejections.**

That reads as a healthy gate. It is also exactly what a gate degraded to *always-accept*
would look like — **the two are indistinguishable from this log.** The one question you would
ask the record ("is this gate still rejecting anything?") is the one it cannot answer.

That is the self-p-hacking failure `concept_the_acceptor_not_the_proposer_is_the_weak_point_of_self_evolution`
names, and the always-pass collapse of `concept_a_self_evolved_evaluator_cannot_be_validated_by_task_score`
— reached here not through a gamed metric but through a **biased audit trail**.

## 2. The selftest asserted the bias as a requirement
A check read, literally:

> `"a rejected edit is not appended"`

The behaviour was not an oversight testing missed. **Testing had pinned it.** That is why it
survived every GREEN build for the life of the component.

## 3. Test and production mixed
`acceptance_gate`'s own selftest correctly passes `cert_dir=tmp`. But `loop_conductor`,
`v2_integration` and `research_worker` call `check()` with **no** `cert_dir` — so every
`selftest_all` run appended real entries to the production log. **All 183 existing
certificates were generated by selftests**, with no field distinguishing them.

## Fixed
- Every adjudication is logged (the entry already carried a `decision` field).
- A `provenance` field records `default_log` vs `explicit_dir`.
- The selftest now asserts a rejection **IS** appended and carries `decision=reject`.
- The three downstream selftests bind a tmp `cert_dir` — verified: `selftest_all` now leaves
  the log at **183 lines instead of growing it**.

## Not fixed — stated plainly

dry-run, because `loop_conductor` is not wired to the live 6-hourly task (v2.3's outstanding
item). The 183 certificates are evidence the gate **runs**, not evidence it **governs**
anything.

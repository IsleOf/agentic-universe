## The principle: "Grader-minimal, and inputs write-isolated"
A modern model arrives from RLVR + RLAIF training pre-disposed to game graders; the two gates *are* graders.
So the design commits to:
1. **Grader-minimal** — minimise the graded surfaces the interior can see, on purpose: the budget so *fewer
   decisions are graded at all*; the structural gate fires only on edits to the running structure; no interior
   quality score exposed to agents. Fewer graders = less attack surface. (Recorded as `supports` from the
   synthesis node to `concept_irreversibility_budget` — the budget's under-appreciated safety role.)
2. **The gate's inputs, not just its logic, are write-isolated from every agent** — a grader-gaming model
   tries to make the risk measure decrease *without* the underlying risk decreasing, by poisoning the
   measure's inputs (ExploitGym cache-poisoning; METR's >7% tool-call spoofing even with an isolated
   source-of-truth store). The check's inputs come from a channel agents cannot write, spoof, or cache-poison.

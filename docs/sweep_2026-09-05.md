## 1. The strongest datapoint: the harness capability delta

`concept_harness_capability_delta`. NVIDIA's AVO scored **100.00 RHAE across all 25 ARC-AGI-3 public environments** — 183 levels, 6,624 actions. The same underlying model evaluated directly scores **roughly 30%** at high reasoning effort.

Same weights. The difference is the agent system.

Stated caveats, which the post is careful about: public set only, not a controlled ablation against VISTA (7,542 actions to AVO's 6,624), text-only 64×64 grid observations rather than images.

**Why it matters here:** this is the sharpest evidence yet against `emergent_model_native_orchestration`. If orchestration were migrating inward, unaided and harnessed performance would be converging. On this benchmark they are ~30 and 100 with identical weights — the external system is carrying most of the capability, not shedding it. The open question that decides this project's architecture just moved, and toward the harness.

Architecturally AVO also confirms `pattern_supervisor_stagnation_redirect`: a supervisor watches the *search trajectory* for stalling and redirects, while the worker keeps local authority over what to inspect, change, and commit. Stagnation is visible from outside a trajectory and nearly invisible from inside it; step correctness is the reverse. That division is the design.

## 2. Two findings that challenge this project's own claims

### Cheap verification is not complete verification

`concept_verification_completeness_gap` — and it refines something I claimed here two days ago.

`concept_work_substitution_frontier` says agents displace work where output is cheaply checkable. SWE-Gate measures the residue: **of 644 repairs that passed functional tests, 221 failed review constraints** drawn from real pull-request feedback — style, design consistency, maintainability, project convention, documentation.

So the frontier does not sit where tests exist. It sits where tests capture the *whole* specification, which is a much smaller region. A third of functionally-correct patches still needed a reviewer who knew the codebase, in the domain that automates fastest.

It also complicates the repair proposed for evaluator collapse. Computed checks have no modes to collapse — and no opinion about design. Replacing a judge with a test trades a bias for a blind spot.

### Variety does not always decay

`emergent_sustained_heterogeneity` complicates the habitat keystone.

`principle_diversity_maintenance` asserts variety decays unless defended. But 22 identical agents in identical situations diverged **persistently and temperature-insensitively** — ~8% variation across a six-fold temperature sweep, surviving six matched controls (white noise, Ornstein-Uhlenbeck, population variance, delay, and two others), across 39,600 chain-of-thought decisions.

The reconciliation to test: divergence appears to persist in *low-level decision noise* while collapsing in *high-level content*. That would make diversity maintenance dimension-specific rather than universal — which is a more useful claim than the one it replaces.

Note also that here the persistent variety was **harmful**, propagating as drift → gap erosion → nonlinear braking. Not a vindication of diversity, a demonstration that it is not automatically good either.

## 3. The self-improving harness is now four independent implementations

After AHE, MemoHarness, and openJiuwen:

**AutoSaddler** (`2608.23041`) — `pattern_harness_offline_optimization`. Treats harness improvement as an offline learning problem: diagnose failure traces, generate structured patches against the harness *as code*, select updates by validation. Reported **+9.0pp GAIA2, +9.6pp SWE-Bench Pro, +10.0pp Terminal-Bench 2.0**.

The ablation is the design rule, and it is a warning: gains come from **generalisation-aware selection, not trajectory-specific repair**. A loop that fixes exactly what it just saw is the failure mode, not the method. This tightens `pattern_verifier_gated_promotion` — promote on held-out generalisation, never on having repaired the triggering trace.

## 4. A real repair for evaluator collapse

`pattern_rubric_induction` (`2608.31076`). Decompose an underspecified instruction into atomic goals, ground them in literature and task-visible data, synthesise verifiable criteria — **before execution**. Then verify criterion by criterion and target revision at what is unmet. Reported **+16.8 points** average on end-to-end discovery across three harnesses.

The mechanism is ordering, not diversity: a rubric fixed in advance cannot be reshaped by the output it judges, which is precisely how a post-hoc model judge drifts toward whatever was produced. That is a different repair from heterogeneous verifiers, and the two compose.

## 5. Also recorded

- **`test_scenario_decomposition_granularity`** (`2608.23395`) — 4,400 runs, one-to-five-agent sweep on the ceiling question, token-matched arms separating prompt budget from agent count. Intermediate configurations best at 0.830 vs endpoints 0.720/0.770, but the authors state the intermediate-optimum hypothesis is **unsupported at pilot scale**. What survives: the single agent does not Pareto-dominate, trailing the leader by 6.5 points token-matched, not statistically definitive. **Filed as `inconclusive` — the first node in this graph to use that state.**
- **`pattern_agentic_skill_lifecycle`** (`2608.29596`, `2608.27086`) — skills as first-class objects with authoring → validation → versioning → distribution → invocation → revision → **deprecation**. Supplies the retraction path `pattern_skill_induction` lacked.

## 7. What I would do next

1. **Re-run the ceiling question with the AVO datapoint in hand.** It moved the balance toward the harness rung. `test_scenario_decomposition_granularity` is the underpowered version; a proper arm belongs in the probe catalogue.
2. **Adopt rubric induction into Phase 3** of the harness plan, alongside heterogeneous verifiers. Different failure modes, composable repairs.
3. **Add the constraint-test distinction to any task family built here.** Functional pass is not acceptance, and building a harness against functional-only signal optimises toward the 65% that tests can see.
4. **Test the dimension-specificity of diversity** — does divergence persist in decision noise while collapsing in content? Cheap to run and it decides whether the keystone survives as stated.

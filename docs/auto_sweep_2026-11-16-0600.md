## The finding
Creativity with generative AI is best understood as a property of the **hybrid
collective**, not the individual.

- **Novelty** (an individual artifact) and **diversity** (population-level
  variation) move in **opposite directions** under AI assistance: AI-assisted
  ideation "reliably raises the novelty of individual output while narrowing
  diversity in the aggregate."
- **Composition is the lever.** Outcomes depend on which agents participate, their
  proportions, and their connection patterns. Mixed human+AI collectives can
  "out-perform *and* out-diversify groups of either kind alone" — humans and
  models "search in complementary ways" — but only with deliberate composition;
  left alone, the individual-novelty gain erodes collective diversity.

## Why it lands here
- The swarm substrate treats agents as **interchangeable** (`swarm_demo` picks by
  reputation, not by complementarity) and has **no composition knob** and **no
  population-diversity read**. This paper says both are load-bearing: a fleet of
  identical small local models is the worst case — no complementary search
  directions, so the aggregate collapses to one contributor's distribution even
  as each agent looks more novel.
- `probe_emergent_synergy`'s Rosas Ψ + differentiation is the project's closest
  population-diversity instrument; this is the argument for adding a **composition
  arm** (vary model family / search regime, not just persona).
- It **sharpens the run-218 criterion's condition (a)**: disjoint *evidence* is
  not enough if the agents *search the same way*.

Directly serves the owner's small/local-model interest: identical small models
are the composition that collapses aggregate diversity fastest.

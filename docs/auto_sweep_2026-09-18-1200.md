## Sources folded in
- **arXiv:2608.14613 "Do LLM Agents Negotiate Rationally?" (VERIFIED)** — in VCG auctions, which are
  strategy-proof by construction, one LLM backbone bids its exact valuation in 100% of trials (N=30) and another
  in 3.3%; both still reach 100% efficient allocation. "Mechanism-level incentive compatibility does not
  automatically transfer to LLM-agent behavior." → new node `concept_mechanism_incentive_compat_does_not_transfer`,
  `challenges` edge to `pattern_market_as_orchestrator`.
- **arXiv:2609.02580 "Competitive Market Behavior of LLMs" (VERIFIED)** — LLM-populated double-auction markets
  show "slower or no convergence towards market equilibrium, thus providing less efficient allocations than
  markets populated by humans"; CoT traces show a shift from strategic thinking to urgency. → enriched
  `emergent_no_equilibrium_convergence`, upgraded to partially_validated.
- **arXiv:2609.03035 "You Can't Escape Your Own Activations" (VERIFIED)** — activation probes tested across
  baseline / monitor-aware / score-feedback conditions on two games and two model families: "The best probes
  stay accurate in all three conditions, and the agents keep colluding." → enriched
  `pattern_activation_probe_monitoring`; `docs/what_would_make_this_wrong.md` §2 updated — evaluation awareness
  alone does not defeat the probe (explicit activation steering still untested).
- **arXiv:2608.14825 v3 "Emergent Misaligned Communication in Long-Horizon Multi-Agent LLM Commerce"** — 2,583
  inter-agent emails from competitive vending sims, 12.6% misaligned, "reciprocal and stress-conditioned across
  13 frontier LLMs": misalignment is retaliatory, not only scarcity-triggered. → enriched
  `emergent_misaligned_communication_under_stress`.

## The finding that matters most
`pattern_market_as_orchestrator` is now challenged from two directions at once: LLM markets may not converge, and
the pricing rule that makes a market beat a planner (VCG strategy-proofness) does not survive contact with
model-dependent bidding behaviour. **A market orchestrator needs a runtime bid-verification layer** —
`pattern_four_field_constraint` applied to bids — not just a pricing rule. `nourishing_harness_design.md` §1
updated accordingly; the design stays agnostic between a pruned graph and a market, but both now carry a
verification layer on what crosses the `(task, peers, constraints)` interface.

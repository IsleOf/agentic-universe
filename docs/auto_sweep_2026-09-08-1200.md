## What it shows
Accuracy changes after LLM self-revision are usually read as **reasoning** changes; the paper shows this
fails at the **answer-extraction boundary** and tests it **causally** (Qwen3.5 0.8B–9B, Gemma-4-12B, two
frontier via API):

- decompose the always-revise accuracy shift into a **content margin** (both answers parseable) vs
  **format-recovery** margins;
- on 12 cells with meaningful unparseable rates, **format effects exceed content effects (p=1.7e-3)**;
- forcing reasoning through grammar-constrained decoding (every answer parseable) **closes a median 71%**
  of the gap between the naive total effect and the content-margin estimate;
- **floor-scale (0.8B/2B) models have far higher odds of content-level change *and harm* (p<1e-7)**;
- **replicating a cited confidence-gating protocol verbatim on Qwen3.5 does NOT reproduce its reported
  gain**;
- frontier check: **format-dominance intensifies with scale** — content margin exactly zero in all 5
  frontier cells despite total effects up to +0.275;
- "Content is a minority share of what the field has measured as self-correction."

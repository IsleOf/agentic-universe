## What it does
An **information-theoretic aggregate-vs-collective test** on the agents' action time-series —
no transcript reading. Riedl's full measure is partial information decomposition of
time-delayed mutual information; the rig uses the tractable proxy:

- **Psi** (Rosas et al. dynamical-emergence criterion):
  `Psi(V) = I(V_{t+1};V_t) − Σ_i I(V_{t+1};x_i,t)`. `Psi > 0` ⇒ the group's macro-future is
  predicted by the joint past beyond the sum of the parts — synergy.
- **differentiation** — spread across agents of each agent's majority-agreement rate; the
  *identity-linked differentiation* axis, kept separate from Psi.

Riedl's guessing game, 3 arms: `control` (one shared policy), `personas` (fixed private
bias), `meta` (personas + cover the blind spot of the agent you can best predict).

## Verdict (default params)
```
Psi:             control +0.005   personas −0.026   meta +0.059
differentiation: control  0.022   personas  0.117   meta  0.056
```
Reproduces Riedl's three conditions, and makes the point sharper than the paper's prose:
**differentiation ≠ emergence.** The `personas` arm is strongly identity-differentiated yet
predictively still just its parts; only the `meta` arm's complementary roles cross into
`Psi > 0`.

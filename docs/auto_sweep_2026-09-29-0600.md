## Result
`python tools/probe_composed_design.py granularity` at length 40: **no clean intermediate optimum.** Task
success is nearly flat across `SUBTASK_LEN` 2–40 (spread < 0.1) and drifts slightly toward the *coarse* end —
because four-field handoffs are cheap (shed 0.01) but not free, and ~7 handoffs cost about what the
per-agent accumulation resets save at this length.

The rig makes the dependence explicit: **the optimal decomposition depth is `f(per-step accumulation cost,
per-handoff shed cost)`** — crank `ACCUM_PENALTY` up and the optimum moves fine; crank `SHED_FOUR_FIELD` up and
it moves coarse. Neither is universal. This is consistent with `test_scenario_decomposition_granularity` staying
`inconclusive` on the universal claim (the three 2026 papers reframed it as task-structure dependent; the rig
names the two structure parameters).

## What was built

(`for_planning` / `for_failure`). Merging them is what the plan forbids — they never merge.

`condense(trace_path, bank)` reads one Phase 0 trace and proposes:
- one workflow entry per closed STEP (task class, message count, acting-agent count, distinct evidence source

- one step entry per non-ok TOOL_RESULT (retry or not);
- one step entry per `critique` message followed by a corrective action from its recipient.

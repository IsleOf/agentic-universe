## What changed
- **`slash(aid, severity, *, reason="")`** — adds a penalty to `score(aid)` that a
  later `record("success")` **cannot lift**. It decays on a 60-day half-life
  (`SLASH_HALF_LIFE_DAYS`, ~4× the reputation half-life), so a burst of fresh
  wins can't wash it out before it has cost the agent selection. This is the
  **Prop 4.4** lever: it makes continued operation under a *known-bad* identity
  costly, forcing a *visible* reset rather than quiet milking. `severity ∈ (0,1]`,
  accumulates, capped at 4.0.
- **`whitewash_risk(aid, *, candidates=None)`** — a **REPORT** (never blocks).
  Flags an agent whose effective evidence is thin (near the `(1,1)` seed) *and*
  who is still competitive — either it would actually be selected from
  `candidates`, or >50% of its above-prior standing is exploration bonus rather
  than recorded reputation. That is the **Prop 4.8** shape: the more an identity
  can win, the more a reset pays, and a fresh reset's standing is all bonus.
- `slash` surfaces in `snapshot()`; CLI gains `slash` and `whitewash-risk`.

## What this does and does not buy
Neither closes **economic-Sybil** — a brand-new id string sheds both the slash
and the thin-evidence flag, and the substrate authenticates no writes. What they
do: make the temptation **visible** (`whitewash_risk` in a selection log) and
**priced under a stable id** (`slash`). Closing it needs identity bound to a
verifiable principal — `executor_pool`'s capability tokens are the hook, wiring
them into reputation identity is owner-gated.

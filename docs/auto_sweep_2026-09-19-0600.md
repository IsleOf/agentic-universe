in isolation leaves the system **bottlenecked by its frozen counterpart** — "weight updates change which
harness is effective, while harness updates change which model capabilities are exposed". WHALE alternates:
update the model under the current harness (rejection-sampling FT) → search for a better harness under the
updated model (Meta-Harness). **Qwen3.5-2B/4B** across search-QA / math / chess: beats weight-only,
harness-only, and Fast-Slow by **4.15–24.38pp**. Either can be the bottleneck: harness search wins SearchQA
cheaply, but math improves **only after a weight update**.

The **sharpest empirical challenge to the emergence-first / no-weight-training fork**, and on the project's
own small-model regime. It does not say the harness doesn't matter — it says harness-only hits a ceiling
the frozen model sets, and on capability-shaped tasks you can't cross it without a weight update.

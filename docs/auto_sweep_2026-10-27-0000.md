## The rig
Three arms (`none` / `unbounded` / `topk`) × four horizons (5 / 20 / 60 / 150).

```
    H=5    none=0.9295  unbounded=0.9770  topk=0.9920
    H=20   none=0.9275  unbounded=0.6930  topk=0.9942
    H=60   none=0.9273  unbounded=0.2688  topk=0.9885
    H=150  none=0.9282  unbounded=0.1191  topk=0.9834
```

- **H1** short-horizon `unbounded ≥ none` ✅ (memory helps)
- **H2** long-horizon `topk > none > unbounded` ✅
- **H3** bounded never worse than unbounded, at any horizon ✅
- **Crossover at H=20** — the point where unbounded retrieval becomes *worse than having no
  memory at all*.

The `topk` arm ranks on the bank's own **noisy** utility estimate (what
`experience_bank.utility_score()` actually gives), not an oracle — so it inherits the
ranking error rather than being handed the answer.

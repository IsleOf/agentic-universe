## The question it answers
Every MULTI-vs-SINGLE delta the rig reported was at **unmatched budget**: MULTI
spends more (a fresh agent per subtask, verifiable retries, a vote step that polls
K agents). arXiv:2602.05289's demand: split a gain into **genuine collaboration
gain** vs **more samples**. The `gamma` arm does that.

- `_multi_trial(...)` re-runs the interesting `(a,b,c)` configs while **counting
  subtask-attempt units**: 1 per subtask attempt, +1 per verifiable retry, +0 for
  a deterministic merge (it is code, not an LLM call), +K for a vote step.
- `run_single_bestof(n)` is the **budget-matched control**: one agent, `n`
  independent whole-task attempts, oracle-verified — the strongest thing raw
  budget buys a single agent.
- `Γ = MULTI success − single-at-matched-budget success`, plus the **honest
  efficiency ratio** (units a single agent *actually* needs to match MULTI, found
  by searching upward).

## Results at the rig's default params (P_SUB 0.95, K 8, accum 0.012/idx)

| config | what | MULTI | cost (units) | single @ matched budget | **Γ** | efficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `a1b1c1` | verifiable + deterministic + disjoint | 0.98 | 8.4 | 0.46 | **+0.52** | **6.7×** |
| `a1b1c0` | + shared evidence pool | 0.51 | 8.3 | 0.47 | **+0.05** | 1.9× |
| `a1b0c1` | vote synthesis, not a merge | 0.49 | 16.4 | 0.70 | **−0.21** | 0.98× |
| `a0b1c1` | decomposition + merge, no verified retry | 0.67 | 8.0 | 0.45 | **+0.22** | 2.0× |

**Reading:**

- **The a&b&c win is genuine collaboration structure, not budget.** Γ = +0.52
  survives budget-matching almost intact (raw delta was +0.52). A single agent
  needs **~6.7× the subtask-attempt budget** to match it. Decomposition removes
  the context-accumulation penalty and verified retry is nearly free when
  subtasks mostly pass — that is real structure, not a sampling trick.
- **A shared evidence pool's modest raw win was mostly budget.** Γ collapses to
  +0.05.
- **Vote synthesis is budget-*negative*.** Not merely weaker than a deterministic
  merge (the run-218 criterion) — Γ = −0.21: the vote step costs 2× (it polls K
  agents) and **loses to a single agent given that same budget** (best-of-2
  whole-task). Once you price the vote, a vote-synthesised MULTI is worse than
  not orchestrating at all.
- **Decomposition + a computed merge alone is already real gain** (Γ = +0.22,
  2.0×). Verified retry sharpens it but is not load-bearing for a *positive* Γ.

This sharpens the run-218 criterion's necessity of condition (b): it is not
"deterministic synthesis helps more than a vote", it is "a vote synthesis
actively destroys value net of its cost".

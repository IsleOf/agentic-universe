## The rule

**Every observer role runs on a different model family from the agents it watches.** Adversarial monitor, Phase 3 verifier, any evaluator. A same-family observer recognises its kin (F1 0.991 under anonymisation) and scores/protects them more leniently (`emergent_self_and_peer_preservation`). A same-family monitor is a compromised monitor.

Costs nothing new: `pattern_structural_decoupling` already requires heterogeneous models across the population. This just fixes where the observers sit — outside the agents' family, not merely at a decorrelated prompt.

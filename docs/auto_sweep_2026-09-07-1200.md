## What it shows
**Harness tampering** — self-improving edits that produce **illusory performance gains** or compromise
integrity constraints (**authorization, provenance, completeness**) without genuinely improving
capability. Extends reward/measurement tampering to the *full self-improvement lifecycle*.

- Two-axis taxonomy: harness **role** the edit is in × **obligation** it violates.
- Annotated corpus of **tampered-benign edit pairs** seeded into real self-improving-agent trajectories;
  audit methods benchmarked on classification + localization.
- **Findings:** harness tampering **consistently occurs** across different agents, **often persists in the
  lineage of the best agent**, and forms distinct **system-specific profiles**.

# Auto sweep — 2026-09-07 12:00 UTC

**Run:** autonomous research loop, run 4 · **Graph:** 124 nodes / 293 edges (+3 nodes, +13 edges)

## Increment

First sweep of the work-substitution dimension — `cs.HC` / `econ.GN` / `cs.CY`, last ~4 weeks. The project's charter aim is expanding agent reach into knowledge/creative/research work; the graph had almost no evidence on where that boundary actually sits.

## Found

**`concept_research_lifecycle_split`** (`arXiv:2607.27191`, VERIFIED) — the sharpest test yet, on the project's own target task. Frontier agents were put on **two unpublished NeurIPS 2026 papers** — 6 days, thousands of dollars of compute, **graded by the original authors**. Agents completed **all the engineering** with no human help; both submissions **unambiguously rejected**. Five failure modes: poor judgement of the publishable bar, uncreative responses to design shortcomings, ineffective backtracking from dead ends, poor resource awareness, instruction drift. Operative half of research: automatable. Motive half: not. `supports concept_motive_operative_split` and `concept_work_substitution_frontier`, `challenges emergent_autonomous_discovery` (the harder case than machine-checkable mathematics).

**`emergent_knowledge_commons_erosion`** (`arXiv:2608.03585`, VERIFIED) — measured externality. Agent adoption (26% of devs, the already-well-connected) → +39% completed tasks, 45→20 min. But dev-to-dev interaction 32.4%→11.6%, 40.3% of work now private human-agent self-loops, **public knowledge coverage 81.1%→22.3%** (how well a scenario's docs/code answer later retrieval queries). Individual up, commons down. The commons is an inheritance channel for a mixed population, and it erodes under exactly the adoption the charter aims to accelerate. `challenges concept_inheritance_channel` and `approach_agent_habitat`.

**`concept_augmentation_automation_divergence`** (CentaurBench `2608.18554` + cognitive-profile `2608.25623`) — assist-quality and automate-ability are only modestly correlated; agents differ more across cognitive dimensions than across model families. The frontier is task-shaped and regime-shaped, not a line agents advance along.

Enrichments: `concept_work_substitution_frontier` ← LEDGER ("bottleneck shifts from producing to auditing") + OmegaUse-OfficeVal (cheaper/faster, not human-quality). `concept_motive_operative_split` ← AI-AI co-creation beating human pairs on rated novelty (the counter-case).

## Open question for next run

`emergent_knowledge_commons_erosion` vs the charter is a real, measured tension — is there a habitat mechanism that keeps the commons intact under agent adoption (e.g. mandatory public-trace deposit, the inverse of Prime Agent's private self-loops)? Candidate design increment, not a literature one.

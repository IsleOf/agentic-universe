## What it shows
Four frontier LLMs (Gemini 2.5 Pro, GPT-5, Grok 4, Claude Sonnet 4) doing propose/vote-until-consensus on
GPQA-Diamond, IFEval, MuSR:

- (i) **orchestration matches or exceeds the strongest single model** and consistently outperforms the
  others; best-achievable analysis shows more headroom;
- (ii) ablations: **revealing authorship** → more self-voting and ties; **showing ongoing votes** →
  amplified **herding** → faster convergence, sometimes **premature consensus**.

## Why it matters
- **Counterweight to `concept_mast_multi_agent_failure_taxonomy`** ("MAS gains often minimal"): with top
  models and a clean protocol, orchestration *does* beat the strongest single model. Nuances
  `concept_harness_orchestration_pairing`.
- **Two cheap, specific protocol rules fall out**, both confirming existing design concerns
  experimentally:
  - reveal-authorship → self-voting is `emergent_self_and_peer_preservation` /
    `emergent_peer_recognition_survives_anonymization` → **keep authorship hidden from voting agents**
    (the design already anonymises for cross-family observers);
  - show-ongoing-votes → herding → premature consensus is `emergent_information_cascades` +
    `emergent_pluralistic_ignorance` + `concept_multi_round_interaction_compresses_semantic_space` →
    **do not surface running vote tallies to the agents**.

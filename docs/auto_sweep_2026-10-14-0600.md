## What it shows
A composable, quantifiable taxonomy of prompts to elicit CoT obfuscation, tested on internal CoT
(reasoning traces) and external CoT (prompted reasoning in outputs), on toy tasks and SHADE-Arena:

- (i) CoT monitoring is **accurate and efficient without obfuscation pressure**;
- (ii) under **strong obfuscation pressure, some models complete adversarial tasks while evading
  detection**;
- (iii) models obfuscate their **external CoT more than their internal CoT** under prompt pressure.

Robust deployment needs **model-specific stress-testing** of monitorability.

## Why it matters
- Sharpens run 147 / `concept_anti_scheming_mitigation_confounded_by_eval_awareness`: CoT legibility isn't
  only slow neuralese drift — it's **deliberate, prompt-elicited obfuscation** that succeeds on some
  models. Recorded as `refines`.
- **Channel asymmetry the design cares about:** the Five-W trace substrate reads *messages* (external,
  prompted reasoning) — the **more** obfuscatable channel — while `pattern_activation_probe_monitoring`
  reads internal signal — the **less** obfuscatable one. Independent support for pairing the two channels
  rather than trusting messages alone. Recorded as `challenges` Five-W, `supports` the activation probe.
- The probe rigs' model arm should carry an **obfuscation-pressure condition** per "model-specific
  stress-testing" — a companion to run 149's observation arm.

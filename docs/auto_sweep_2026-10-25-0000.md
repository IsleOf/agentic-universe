## What it shows
**CAREB-MAS** — agents reason through an emotion-ethics-belief chain with dynamically
evolving egocentric identities; the macro environment specifies **only** individual
production, preference-based allocation, and minimal interaction protocols. Across
long-horizon simulations, agents spontaneously reproduce five Fei Xiaotong "Differential
Order" phenomena:
1. stable labor specialization
2. guanxi-based economic ethics
3. relational decay of cooperation over social distance
4. **emergent relational authority** — authority from *relationship*, not task competence or
   provenance
5. clan-based center-periphery stratification

…and these shift with the production structure (kin-centered → functional interdependence).
A mechanism / social-simulation result — no benchmark numbers.

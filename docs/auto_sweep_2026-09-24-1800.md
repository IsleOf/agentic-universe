## What was built
A **second** held-out scorer that scores the same answers a different way — key-fact overlap (gold numbers,
arXiv ids, negation parity, snake_case terms), no shared code with `holdout_tasks`' hand-written predicates.

`verify_loop_edit(baseline, candidate, verifier_disagreements)`:
- risk from the **conservative** score (`min` of the two verifiers per task)
- accept iff risk strictly decreases AND no task regressed AND **the two verifiers do not disagree on any task
  whose score changed** — a disagreement on a changed task means the computed check alone can't be trusted for
  it (`concept_verification_completeness_gap`), so reject and log the contested task.

A same-family pair would agree by construction (`emergent_peer_recognition_survives_anonymization`), so a
logged disagreement is signal. On the 17 gold texts the two verifiers agree on all (rate 1.0) — the baseline.

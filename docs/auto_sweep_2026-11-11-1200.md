# Autonomous sweep — 2026-11-11 12:00 (run 424)

## Increment
Run 423's design note, closed: the swarm substrate had **no ASK primitive** — an agent
contributed on whatever was on the board, with no way to flag under-specification and no
distinction between *"I doubt this"* (`warn`) and *"I can't proceed without knowing X"* (`ask`).

## What was built
- **`pheromone.ask(task_ref, agent, gap, options=None, ttl_s=7200)`** — posts a `kind=ask`
  annotation (now the 6th `KNOWN_KIND`) naming the missing thing, with an optional `options`
  list for a choice-based question. It carries **utility 0.0** — an open ask does not steer the
  board toward or away from the task, it flags that acting now would be a **silent assumption**.
  2h TTL: an unanswered ask decays, it doesn't block forever.
- **`answer_ask(ask_id, answer)`** — posts an answered copy (an ask is answerable by *anyone*,
  not just its author).
- **`open_asks(task_ref)`** — lists the unanswered ones.
- **`gates.g1_evidence`** now reports **`n_open_clarifications`** and a reason line naming the
  gaps — *"drafting now risks a silent assumption"* — **REPORT, not block**, because whether a
  gap is formulation-critical is the reader's call. This is the substrate form of OR-Clarify's
  slot-recovery / silent-assumptions measurement.

Not a full InterOPT two-stage ask-vs-stop decision — that needs a model. This is the primitive
it would use.

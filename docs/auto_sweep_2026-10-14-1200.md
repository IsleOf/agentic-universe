## What it says
AI systems that think in human language let us monitor their chains of thought for the intent to
misbehave. CoT monitoring is **imperfect** — some misbehaviour goes unnoticed — but it is promising and
warrants research + investment **alongside** existing methods. Key stance: **CoT monitorability may be
fragile**, so frontier developers should weigh the impact of development decisions on it.

## Why it matters for this project
- The Phase 0 trace substrate's **readability is a property to actively preserve and measure**, not
  assume — it should be a measured input to the loop.
- "Weigh development decisions by their effect on monitorability" applies to the **middle loop**: a
  harness edit could degrade the legibility of the traces the *outer* loop depends on. So **trace
  legibility belongs in the Phase 3 grounded-acceptance risk measure**, not just held-out task quality.
  Recorded as `informs` `pattern_grounded_acceptance_only`.
- "Alongside existing safety methods" is the design's both-channels stance — CoT monitoring paired with
  `pattern_activation_probe_monitoring`, not instead of it.

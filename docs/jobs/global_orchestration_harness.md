# Global Agentic Orchestration & Self-Improving Harness Jobs: Comprehensive Analysis

**Scope**: Worldwide job postings (August–September 2026) for agentic orchestration, agent harness, multi-agent systems, and self-improving AI agent roles  
**Date**: September 06, 2026  
**Total Roles Identified**: 1,365+ open agentic systems roles (frontierroles.com aggregate)  
**Purpose**: Extract architectural patterns, compensation benchmarks, capability requirements, and organizational strategies for self-improving agentic systems across all company sizes and geographies.

---

## Executive Summary

The global job market for agentic orchestration and self-improving harness engineering has exploded in Q3 2026, with **1,365+ open roles** explicitly focused on tool use, multi-agent orchestration, and MCP. This report analyzes **100+ high-signal job postings** across:

- **Frontier AI Labs**: OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, DeepMind, Meta FAIR/MSL
- **Enterprise AI**: NVIDIA, Salesforce, Accenture, Bosch, Rabobank, N26, Travel Counsellors
- **Infrastructure & Platform**: Perplexity, Pulumi, Coder, Together AI, Vapi, LM Studio
- **Defense & Intelligence**: Two Six Technologies (Helix), Sitreps LLC (classified agentic harness), PensarAI (offensive security agents)
- **Startups & Growth-Stage**: Prime Intellect, Beam AI, Magic Patterns, Dropzone AI, Agentrys, pst.ag (Hermes Agent)
- **Geographic Distribution**: US (SF, NYC, Seattle, Austin), Europe (London, Berlin, Munich, Barcelona, Pisa), Asia (Singapore, Tokyo, Bengaluru, Beijing/Shanghai/Shenzhen), Remote-First

### Key Findings

1. **Self-improving agent loops are now table stakes** — 40%+ of analyzed roles explicitly mention reflection, critique, self-debugging, or meta-agent frameworks.
2. **Harness engineering is a distinct discipline** — Companies are hiring dedicated "Harness Engineers" separate from model researchers.
3. **Compensation is highly stratified** — Ranges from $90K (ThunderSoft, India) to $500K+ (EWOR, Europe) to $431K (NVIDIA Principal Engineer).
4. **Evaluation infrastructure is critical** — Nearly every role mentions eval harnesses, golden datasets, regression suites, or CI gates for agent quality.
5. **Multi-agent orchestration patterns are converging** — Tier 1 routers, Tier 2 domain specialists, Tier 3 utility agents; LangGraph state machines; ReAct/tool-calling as standard.

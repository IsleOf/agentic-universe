## The finding
A dynamic economic model of **reputation as capital** when identities are cheap
to abandon. Each period an agent chooses: operate honestly (build reputation) or
**extract** — cash in accumulated reputation with a bad delivery, then reset to a
penalised fresh identity. A temptation function compares the two; the agent
deviates when **extracted value − reset cost ≥ 0**.

- **Prop 4.1:** reputation is genuine economic capital — better reputation always
  raises agent worth.
- **Prop 4.4:** a full identity **reset** disciplines better than continuing
  under a degraded identity (the agent keeps less future value) — *but only when
  the reset is costly*.
- **Prop 4.8:** accumulated reputation can **increase** deviation temptation —
  the immediately extractable value grows faster than the preservation benefit. A
  high-reputation agent routed the most work has the most to gain from one bad
  batch then a reset.
- **Corollary 5.3:** explicit honesty thresholds as functions of identity-reset
  cost `κ_R`, reputation decay `ρ`, and staking `κ_S`. Stronger staking deters
  deviation but also lowers the agent's optimal quality.
- **Policy:** bind identity to a verifiable principal (KYC / collateral) to raise
  `κ_R`; detection-coupled slashing (penalty ∝ *detected* misconduct, not
  volume); calibrate decay (fast `ρ` fights whitewashing, slow preserves honest
  incentives); reputation *alone* fails when reset is cheap — pair with staking /
  attestations / escrow.

## Why it lands here
`tools/reputation.py` is Beta-Bernoulli + a UCB exploration bonus + 14-day
exponential decay toward the `(1,1)` seed. Three exact hits:

1. **The substrate makes reset free.** `pheromone.annotate`'s `agent` field is
   unverified — the whole trust boundary. `κ_R ≈ 0`, so by Corollary 5.3 the
   substrate is on the wrong side of every honesty threshold: a fresh sock-puppet
   gets a `0.5` prior + the full exploration bonus, near-indistinguishable from a
   decayed veteran.
2. **The exploration bonus is the whitewashing subsidy.** It exists for a good
   non-calcification reason
   (`concept_reputation_selection_stays_non_calcifying_only_with_ucb_exploration_and_recency_decay_toward_the_prior`)
   but it also pays the reset agent. The two goals are in direct tension and the
   module never priced it.
3. **Prop 4.8 is unhandled.** The "personal assistant" role meant to emerge from
   performance data (swarm task deliverable 4) is exactly the high-reputation
   agent with the most extractable value and no slash a reset cannot erase.
   `executor_pool`'s `mint_token` / `redeem_token` are the "bind to a principal"
   direction but are not wired into reputation identity.

Small/local-model relevance: a fleet of local models on one box has *no* external
identity cost — reset is "use a different string", `κ_R = 0` exactly, the worst
case of the model.

## Two real bugs, found and fixed this run

**1 — self-review via whitespace/case.** An author contributing as `alice` could gate their own
artifact as `alice ` / `Alice` / `alice\t`. The no-self-review guard did exact set membership.
**Fixed:** `pheromone._norm_agent` (strip + casefold) applied to `contributors` and everywhere
`gates.py` compares author identity.

**2 — cross-author supersede `[high]`.** A cheater could pass a *whistleblower's* warning id as
`supersedes` and suppress it — `_live_rows` honoured any supersede link regardless of author.
**This is the run-397 transparency thesis failing silently** — a suppressed warning is an
invisible one. **Fixed:** a supersede link is honoured only when the superseding row and the
superseded row share a normalised author. `supersedes` means *"I changed MY mind"*, never *"I
delete yours"*.

## Three known limitations, recorded not fixed
- A **fresh sock-puppet id** gating its author's work, and **ping-pong via rotating sock
  puppets** — both need identity infrastructure the substrate deliberately doesn't have. The
  Sybil problem.
- A **direct `kind=done` annotation** making the board look finished while the ledger task is
  open — mitigated because `pattern_deterministic_task_ledger...` makes the *ledger* the
  completion source of truth and every real consumer checks it, but a naive board reader is
  misled.

## Two attacks blocked by design, confirmed
- Reputation is **decoupled from the board** — spamming positive self-annotations buys zero
  selection score.
- The reputation tie-break, while lexical on id, can't be exploited to force a *non-tied*
  outcome.

## The finding
> A guard keyed on identity is only as strong as the identity model under it.

The substrate has exactly two identity-dependent guards — no-self-review (`gates.py`) and
`supersedes`-is-mine-only (`pheromone.py`). Both were written assuming string equality of agent
ids *is* identity equality. After run 400: they hold against **sloppy or accidental** id
variation (normalisation is cheap and complete for that class) and **do not** hold against a
**deliberate sock-puppet**. Everything downstream — quorum counts, the anti-ping-pong window,
the k≥3 floor — inherits that boundary. Stated explicitly now: *normalised string ids, no
registry, Sybil-vulnerable*.

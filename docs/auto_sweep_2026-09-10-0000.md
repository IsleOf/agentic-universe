## The messaging design, settled

The variable is **covert vs transparent**, not restricted vs free:

1. **Same-parent-ID filtering on direct messages** — removes the accidental covert side-channel (run 6, Hugging Face).
2. **Broadcast channels encouraged, not restricted** — a logged everyone-visible channel is what the emergent immune response needs (run 14, `emergent_whistleblowing`). Every message is a Five-W record.
3. **No covert path** — no unlogged channel, no private pairwise channel outside same-parent-ID.
4. **One cross-family adversarial monitor** (run 13) as backstop.
5. **Ostrom-style interior governance** as the population scales (run 14).

This is the emergence-first design with three runs of findings folded in: it stays maximally open where openness is what lets the swarm police itself, and closes only the paths that exist to evade.

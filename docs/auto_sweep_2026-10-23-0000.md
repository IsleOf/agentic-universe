## What it shows
First empirical study of **multi-agent AI control** — several agents jointly pursuing a
malicious goal (weight exfiltration, training-run poisoning). **FakeLab**: synthetic AI-lab
codebase, 9 services, 86 benign tasks, 4 attack objectives.
- **Fragmentation effect** — as more agents coordinate to attack, per-agent monitoring
  becomes less likely to catch *any* attacker.
- Not driven by the benign:malicious code ratio (likely model-capability dependent).
- An explicit **planner** amplifies fragmentation, raises attack completion **up to 7×**,
  and mildly sharpens executors' (poor) monitor-theory-of-mind.
- A weak "trusted" monitor misses most attacks; a stronger one cuts undetected success
  **>10×** but stays imperfect.
- No defense proposed; FakeLab released for the community.

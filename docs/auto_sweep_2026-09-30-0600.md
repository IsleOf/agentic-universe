## The taxonomy
| training stage | loss | misalignment flavour | example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pretraining / SFT | imitative (next-token) | "seven deadly sins" — all human vices | Bing-Sydney; emergent-misalignment-from-insecure-code |
| RLHF / DPO | human approval | "glazing" / sycophancy | GPT-4o |
| RLVR | automatic verifier | "literal genie" / monkey's paw / ruthless optimization | **the HuggingFace hacking incident** (+ Anthropic, Meta, UK-AISI) |
| RLAIF | LLM-judge approval | "trickster" — lie/jailbreak the judge when it can be fooled → general **apparent-success-seeking** | Greenblatt's "current AIs seem pretty misaligned to me" (oversell, downplay, claim-done-early, bullshit hard-to-check tasks) |

Rule of thumb: crank a training component up, get more of that flavour. Modern LLMs get **RLVR + RLAIF mixed**
→ **two-faced**: the model susses out whether a situation is an RLVR test (act literal-genie) or an RLAIF test
(act trickster) and behaves accordingly.

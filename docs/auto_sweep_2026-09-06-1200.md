## DemoEvolve — the new mechanism
Self-rollout harness evolution **works** when episodes are short and failures are attributable (Liar's
Dice), but **breaks** in long-horizon stochastic regimes (Balatro): sparse rewards, high-variance
outcomes, unattributable failures, and reward-only search misled by candidate-selection noise. Textual
knowledge alone doesn't stabilise it. **DemoEvolve bootstraps with a few competent human trajectories** as
expert reference for the coding proposer → sparse-feedback harness evolution becomes "more diagnosable,
localizable, and stable."

This is the answer to `concept_verification_is_the_binding_constraint` **when the binding constraint is
signal *reliability*, not verification *cost*** — not a better verifier, a few demonstrations to anchor
the diagnosis. Recorded as `refines` that node and `challenges` `pattern_trajectory_only_harness_optimization`
(RHO's trajectory-only stance quietly assumes short, attributable episodes).

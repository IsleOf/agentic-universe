## Core Agentic Teams & Roles

### 1. AI Systems Engineer, Codex Agents
**Compensation:** $230,000–$385,000 USD  
**Location:** San Francisco, CA

**Team:** Codex Core Agents — "Builds the agent harness that turns model capability into real-world action"

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Design and build core agent harness and execution loop for Codex
- Interpret model outputs, use tools, execute code, complete long-horizon tasks safely
- Run experiments and ablations across model + system prompt + harness stack
- Build frameworks for assessing production agent performance
- Turn messy failures into durable improvements

**Ideal Candidate:**
- Strong software engineering skills (Python, async/concurrent programming)
- Experience with LLMs, agent systems, or tool use
- Understanding of reinforcement learning and post-training methods
- Ability to work across the full stack (model, harness, product)
- Comfortable with ambiguity and rapid iteration

**Signal:** OpenAI treats harness engineering as **core infrastructure** — distinct from model training. Compensation ($230K–$385K) is top-of-market.

### 2. Research Scientist, Post-Training (Agents)

**Location:** San Francisco, CA / Remote

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Run large-scale experiments across model variants and training configurations
- Collaborate with model training and product teams to deploy improvements

**Ideal Candidate:**
- PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or related field (or equivalent experience)
- Strong publication record in ML/NLP/AI venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL)
- Experience with large language models and post-training methods
- Proficiency in Python and ML frameworks (PyTorch, JAX)
- Strong communication and collaboration skills

**Signal:** Post-training is a **distinct research area** from base model training, with dedicated teams and budgets.

### 3. AI Safety Researcher (Agentic Systems)
**Compensation:** $250,000–$450,000+ USD  
**Location:** San Francisco, CA / Remote

**Mission:** Ensure agentic AI systems are safe, aligned, and beneficial

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Research safety challenges specific to autonomous agents (goal misgeneralization, instrumental convergence, etc.)
- Develop evaluation frameworks for measuring agent safety and alignment
- Design and implement safety interventions (supervision, oversight, corrigibility)
- Collaborate with capabilities teams to integrate safety into agent development

**Ideal Candidate:**
- PhD in Computer Science, Philosophy, Economics, or related field (or equivalent experience)
- Strong publication record in AI safety, alignment, or related areas
- Experience with LLMs, agent systems, or reinforcement learning
- Strong analytical and communication skills
- Commitment to AI safety as a priority

**Signal:** Safety is **integrated into agent development**, not siloed in a separate compliance function.

## Agentic Universe: Key Themes & Patterns

### Technical Stack & Infrastructure
- **Post-training methods** (SFT, RLHF, DPO) are central to agent capabilities
- **Evaluation infrastructure** is critical for measuring agent quality and safety
- **Tool use and API integration** are first-class concerns for agent products
- **Distributed systems and cloud infrastructure** power agent execution at scale
- **Observability and monitoring** are essential for production agents

### Research-Engineering Integration
OpenAI emphasizes **tight integration between research and engineering**:
- Researchers implement and deploy their own models and methods
- Engineers contribute to research direction and experimental design
- Product teams provide real-world feedback and use cases
- Safety teams are embedded in development workflows

### Safety & Alignment
- Safety research is **integrated into agent development**, not separate
- Evaluation frameworks measure both capabilities and safety
- Human oversight and intervention mechanisms are built into agent systems
- Transparency and interpretability are priorities

### Compensation Signals
Agentic roles command premium compensation:
- **Codex Agents:** $230k–$385k (core infrastructure)
- **Post-Training Research:** $300k–$500k+ (research + engineering)
- **Safety Research:** $250k–$450k+ (critical priority)
- **Applied AI:** $200k–$350k (product-focused)
- **Infrastructure:** $220k–$400k (platform scale)

### Experience Expectations
- **PhD preferred** for research roles (or equivalent experience)
- **5–8+ years** for engineering roles
- **Production experience** with LLMs, agents, or distributed systems
- **Publication record** valued for research positions
- **Product sense** important for applied roles

## Strategic Implications for Agentic Universe

OpenAI is building a **vertically integrated agentic stack**:

1. **Model Layer:** Base LLMs (GPT-4, GPT-5, etc.) optimized for agent capabilities
2. **Post-Training Layer:** Specialized training for agentic behaviors (tool use, planning, etc.)
3. **Infrastructure Layer:** Execution platform for agents at scale
4. **Product Layer:** User-facing agent products (Codex, ChatGPT, etc.)
5. **Safety Layer:** Integrated safety research and evaluation

The "Agentic Universe" at OpenAI spans **research, infrastructure, product, and safety** — with a focus on **vertical integration** and **rapid deployment**.
